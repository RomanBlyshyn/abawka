2. How buying works

  1. The website is a catalogue. Adding products to the cart and sending a request is not yet a purchase: it is a request for the seller to contact you. Sending a request is free and does not oblige you to buy anything.
  2. After receiving your request, the seller contacts you by the method you chose (phone, Telegram, Viber, WhatsApp or email) and confirms availability, the total price, the delivery method and cost, and how to pay.
  3. The contract of sale is concluded when you accept this offer from the seller, for example by replying that you agree or by paying. The seller then sends you the agreed terms in writing (by message or email).
  4. The products are reserved for you while the seller handles your request. If the request is cancelled, the reservation is released.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices on the website are final prices of the products, shown in currency. Prices in other currencies are approximate and shown for information only.
  • Prices do not include delivery. Delivery is paid by the buyer; the seller tells you the exact delivery cost before you agree to buy.
  • When a price reduction is shown, the website also shows the lowest price of the product in the 30 days before the reduction.
  • One promo code can be used per request. The discount is calculated by our server and shown in the cart before you send the request. Promo codes cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • There is no online payment on the website. You pay the seller directly, by the method agreed with the seller. The website never asks for your card details.
